Single-line input. Two shapes: 14px-radius fields inside dialogs, fully-pill search boxes over the map.

\`\`\`jsx
<TextField label="Nombre de la ubicación" defaultValue="Cornellà del Terri" />
<TextField tone="glass" shape="pill" placeholder="Buscar lugar…" />
\`\`\`

Labels are 11px \`--ink-soft\` above the field — Plou never uses floating/inline Material labels. Placeholders end in an ellipsis character.
